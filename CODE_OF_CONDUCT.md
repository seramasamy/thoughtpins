# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Thought Pins community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for users and the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention,
trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, publishing another person's private
information, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in
a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that
conflict with this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict a
participant whose behavior is harmful, threatening, or persistently disruptive.

Report conduct concerns through the repository host's private maintainer contact
or moderation channel. Do not include personal information in a public issue.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with safety and due
process considered for everyone involved.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person is officially representing
the project in public spaces.
