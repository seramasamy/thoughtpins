The honest verdict: the current mark's idea was never the problem — its small-size execution is. L1 keeps the brain-pin meaning (memory + place, the actual product concept) and fixes legibility, so it is the recommendation. L2 ties it on points: if you want the boldest, simplest possible mark and are willing to let the brain go, it is the strongest minimalist identity of the set — but a plain pin is easier to confuse with every other location icon. L3 is the most beautiful at large sizes and the least usable at small ones. L4 is elegant and utterly interchangeable.

Scores are judgment, not measurement — the size ladders above are the real evidence. Trust your eye at 16px.
