Why it feels different

A calmer way to remember

Writing something down should not feel like filing it. Thought Pins turns ordinary conversation into a record you can walk back through, without turning your life into data entry.

  • Yours to take Export everything as an Obsidian-compatible vault of plain Markdown files. No lock-in, readable without us.
  • Yours to end Delete your account and its data from inside the app, whenever you decide.
  • Yours alone Kept to your account. Never used to build an advertising profile.
