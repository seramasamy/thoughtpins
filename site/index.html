Your control

A personal record.
Your say in what stays.

Your memories deserve clear choices. Export your record, choose what enters AI processing, and delete your account from the app.

  • Yours to take Download your account data. On the web, export an Obsidian-compatible Markdown vault, too. Third-party source text follows the export policy.
  • Yours to end Delete your account and its data from inside the app, whenever you decide.
  • Yours to choose AI processing requires your consent. Your content is scoped to your account and is never used to build an advertising profile. How AI processing works
