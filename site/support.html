Help and reporting

Support

Product, account, privacy, deletion, security, and AI safety support

For general help, email support@thoughtpins.com. Include the app platform, approximate time, and request ID when available, but do not send private journal text, passwords, or access tokens.

Account help

Use the app Account screen to update preferences, export your data, sign out, or delete the account. Connected devices and sessions are managed in the web app. If you cannot sign in, email support from the address associated with your account. For account deletion, follow the public Delete Account resource.

Forgot your password

Password reset is handled by support. Email support@thoughtpins.com from the email or phone associated with your account and ask to reset your password — the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in screen opens this message for you. Support verifies that you control the account before making any change. Never send your existing password; if you have lost it, it is reset, not looked up.

Privacy and data requests

For access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion questions, contact privacy@thoughtpins.com. Thought Pins may ask for limited verification before releasing or deleting account information.

AI safety reports

Report unsafe AI output, harmful or illegal content, copyright concerns, harassment, privacy concerns, or deceptive behavior through the in-app report control or support@thoughtpins.com. Include the approximate time, platform, and request ID if available. Do not include private journal text unless support provides an appropriate channel.

Security or privacy incidents

Suspected vulnerabilities or unauthorized access should be reported privately to security@thoughtpins.com. Do not publicly disclose account data or test production accounts without authorization. See the Security page for reporting guidance.

Source and copyright concerns

Thought Pins is designed for content the user is authorized to save. To report a source-rights or copyright concern, email legal@thoughtpins.com with the source URL, a description of the work, the affected content, and contact information.

Service interruptions

During maintenance, the app may display a banner and pause new chat, journal, or import operations. Avoid repeatedly resubmitting the same content. Existing local drafts remain available where supported, and the app can be retried after the stated maintenance window.

Emergency and professional help

Thought Pins is not an emergency service and does not provide medical, legal, financial, or crisis support. Contact local emergency services or a qualified professional when immediate or professional assistance is needed.
