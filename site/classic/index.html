Control is part of the product

Your memories should not become an ad profile.

Thought Pins is designed without a public feed, advertising profile, or cross-app ad tracking. AI processing and source imports are disclosed, and important decisions remain yours.

  • Account-level tenant isolation
  • Data export, including an Obsidian-compatible vault on the web
  • In-app and web account deletion paths
  • Source provenance and AI disclosure
